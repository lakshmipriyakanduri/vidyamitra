Privacy and safety

What is saved on your device: your class, roll number, quiz results and text size. These stay in this browser or phone and are never sent to our server or the AI.

What is sent to the AI: only the topic, class level and the text you type, to Google Gemini through our server, to get an answer. We do not keep a list of your questions. Please never type personal details.

Not a password: anyone can type any class and roll number on a shared device, so do not use this as a secure login.

Safety: the app only helps with school topics. AI can make mistakes, so check important answers with a teacher or textbook. If something worries you, talk to a parent, teacher, or trusted adult. CHILDLINE 1098 is free in India.

Parents and teachers: sample topics are not the official syllabus. Please review them. Contact the app owner for questions or changes.
